Privacy Policy

Learn how TV Internet US handles information collected when you use our website.

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TV Internet US. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information may be collected and used when you visit our website.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected through TV Internet US and how that information may be used.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect or receive certain types of information.

Information You Provide

If you contact us through our contact page, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

ZIP Code Information

Our website may allow you to enter a ZIP code to view estimated internet or TV provider availability in an area.

ZIP code information may be used to display relevant provider results and does not necessarily identify a specific individual.

Automatically Collected Information

Like many websites, certain technical information may be collected automatically when you visit the website. This may include information such as browser type, device type, referring pages, general usage information, and approximate location information.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through the website may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and improving the website.
  • Displaying provider and service information based on searches submitted by visitors.
  • Responding to questions, feedback, or requests sent to us.
  • Understanding how visitors use the website.
  • Improving website content, functionality, and user experience.
  • Detecting security issues or preventing misuse of the website.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TV Internet US or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage, or support advertising and affiliate tracking.

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. You can generally manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand general website traffic and visitor behavior. These services may collect information about how visitors interact with the website.

Analytics information is generally used to improve the website and understand which content is useful to visitors.

6. Affiliate Links and Third-Party Websites

TV Internet US may participate in affiliate marketing programs. This means that we may receive compensation when a visitor clicks certain links or completes an eligible action with a third-party provider.

When you click a link to an internet, TV, wireless, or other third-party provider, you may leave the TV Internet US website and be subject to that provider's own privacy policy and terms.

We do not control the privacy practices, policies, or security of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for purposes such as analytics, website hosting, advertising, affiliate tracking, forms, or other website functionality.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

8. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information submitted directly to us through our contact form.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve the website.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of TV Internet US, our visitors, or others.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information that may be collected through the website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

TV Internet US is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

11. Your Choices

Depending on your browser and device settings, you may be able to control cookies and certain tracking technologies.

You may also choose not to provide information through our contact form. However, doing so may prevent us from responding to your inquiry.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
