Common Questions

Is checking availability free?

Yes. You can use the ZIP-code comparison without creating an account or paying a fee to use the checker.

Does a ZIP code guarantee service at my address?

No. ZIP-code results are a starting point. Exact service availability, pricing, speeds, equipment and promotions can vary by address and should be confirmed with the provider.

What if I don't see the provider I want?

Provider availability varies by location. You can also browse our state and provider guides for additional information.

Do you sell my information?
