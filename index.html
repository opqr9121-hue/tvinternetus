Common questions

Is this really free?

Yes. Checking availability at your ZIP code never costs anything and doesn't require creating an account.

How accurate are the results?

Results are based on provider coverage data at the ZIP-code level. Exact plans, pricing, and speeds should always be confirmed directly with the provider for your specific address.

What if no providers show up?

Some ZIP codes may not yet be in our data set. Try again shortly, or contact us directly and we'll help you find options in your area.

Do you sell my information?

No. We don't require an account, collect personal data beyond your ZIP code search, or sell your information to third parties.
