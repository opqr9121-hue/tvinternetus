Download Speed

Download speed measures how quickly information can be received from the internet. Higher speeds are useful for streaming, downloads and multiple connected devices.

Upload Speed

Upload speed measures how quickly information can be sent to the internet. It matters for video calls, cloud backups and uploading large files.

Ping

Ping measures the response time between your device and a test server. Lower ping is generally better for gaming and real-time applications.
