Frequently Asked Questions — Arkansas Internet

What's the state of internet access in Arkansas?

FCC Broadband Data Collection figures put availability in Arkansas at around 38%, a middle-of-the-pack number — solid in Little Rock but noticeably weaker once you leave the metro area.

How many internet providers cover Arkansas?

About 5 major providers are reported active in Arkansas per FCC data, concentrated mostly around Little Rock and other population centers.

Is rural Arkansas well-served for broadband?

Not as well as the metro areas — rural parts of Arkansas commonly depend on fixed wireless or satellite service where wired broadband hasn't been built out.
