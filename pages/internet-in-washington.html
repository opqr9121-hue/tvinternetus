Frequently Asked Questions — Washington Internet

Is Washington well-covered for internet access?

Yes, relative to most states — FCC Broadband Data Collection figures put fiber/high-speed availability in Washington at around 56%, among the stronger numbers nationally, with Seattle as the strongest-served area.

How many major internet providers serve Washington?

FCC data identifies roughly 10 major providers active in Washington, giving most residents in and around Seattle more than one realistic option to compare.

Where in Washington is coverage weakest?

Even in higher-coverage states like Washington, availability drops outside the main metro corridors — rural counties typically rely on fixed wireless or satellite rather than the wired options common in Seattle.
