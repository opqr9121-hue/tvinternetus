Frequently Asked Questions — North Carolina Internet

What's the state of internet access in North Carolina?

FCC Broadband Data Collection figures put availability in North Carolina at around 53%, a middle-of-the-pack number — solid in Charlotte but noticeably weaker once you leave the metro area.

How many internet providers cover North Carolina?

About 8 major providers are reported active in North Carolina per FCC data, concentrated mostly around Charlotte and other population centers.

Is rural North Carolina well-served for broadband?

Not as well as the metro areas — rural parts of North Carolina commonly depend on fixed wireless or satellite service where wired broadband hasn't been built out.
