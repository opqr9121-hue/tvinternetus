Frequently Asked Questions — Illinois Internet

What's the state of internet access in Illinois?

FCC Broadband Data Collection figures put availability in Illinois at around 54%, a middle-of-the-pack number — solid in Chicago but noticeably weaker once you leave the metro area.

How many internet providers cover Illinois?

About 8 major providers are reported active in Illinois per FCC data, concentrated mostly around Chicago and other population centers.

Is rural Illinois well-served for broadband?

Not as well as the metro areas — rural parts of Illinois commonly depend on fixed wireless or satellite service where wired broadband hasn't been built out.
