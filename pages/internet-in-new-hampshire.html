Frequently Asked Questions — New Hampshire Internet

What's the state of internet access in New Hampshire?

FCC Broadband Data Collection figures put availability in New Hampshire at around 41%, a middle-of-the-pack number — solid in Manchester but noticeably weaker once you leave the metro area.

How many internet providers cover New Hampshire?

About 6 major providers are reported active in New Hampshire per FCC data, concentrated mostly around Manchester and other population centers.

Is rural New Hampshire well-served for broadband?

Not as well as the metro areas — rural parts of New Hampshire commonly depend on fixed wireless or satellite service where wired broadband hasn't been built out.
