Internet Options in Texas

Fiber Internet

Fiber can provide very high download and upload speeds and is often attractive for households with gaming, streaming, large uploads and remote work needs.

Cable Internet

Cable networks can provide broad coverage and fast download speeds across many metropolitan and suburban areas.

5G Home Internet

Fixed wireless and 5G home internet can provide an alternative to wired service in locations where the network supports a customer's address.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best internet provider in Texas?

There is no single best provider for every Texas household. The best option depends on which networks serve your specific address, the speeds you need and the available pricing and promotions.

Does Texas have fiber internet?

Yes. Fiber service is available in many Texas communities, although availability varies by provider and exact address.

How do I find internet providers at my address?

Enter your ZIP code above to see provider options. Then use the provider's own availability checker to confirm service at your exact address.

Is 5G home internet available everywhere in Texas?

No. 5G home internet availability depends on network coverage, capacity and the exact service address.

Can I get internet without a cable connection?

Depending on your location, alternatives can include fiber, fixed wireless, 5G home internet and satellite internet.
