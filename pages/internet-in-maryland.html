Frequently Asked Questions — Maryland Internet

Is Maryland well-covered for internet access?

Yes, relative to most states — FCC Broadband Data Collection figures put fiber/high-speed availability in Maryland at around 60%, among the stronger numbers nationally, with Baltimore as the strongest-served area.

How many major internet providers serve Maryland?

FCC data identifies roughly 6 major providers active in Maryland, giving most residents in and around Baltimore more than one realistic option to compare.

Where in Maryland is coverage weakest?

Even in higher-coverage states like Maryland, availability drops outside the main metro corridors — rural counties typically rely on fixed wireless or satellite rather than the wired options common in Baltimore.
