Frequently Asked Questions — Louisiana Internet

What's the state of internet access in Louisiana?

FCC Broadband Data Collection figures put availability in Louisiana at around 43%, a middle-of-the-pack number — solid in New Orleans but noticeably weaker once you leave the metro area.

How many internet providers cover Louisiana?

About 4 major providers are reported active in Louisiana per FCC data, concentrated mostly around New Orleans and other population centers.

Is rural Louisiana well-served for broadband?

Not as well as the metro areas — rural parts of Louisiana commonly depend on fixed wireless or satellite service where wired broadband hasn't been built out.
