How to Choose the Right Provider

Start with what's actually available at your address, not what's nationally popular — fiber from AT&T or Verizon Fios outperforms cable where it's offered, but cable (Xfinity, Spectrum, Optimum) still covers far more addresses. If you want TV without a traditional cable bundle, DIRECTV Stream works over any existing internet connection. In areas without reliable wired options, T-Mobile's wireless network can also serve as a home internet substitute.
