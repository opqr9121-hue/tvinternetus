Frequently Asked Questions — South Dakota Internet

Why is internet coverage lower in South Dakota?

FCC Broadband Data Collection figures show availability in South Dakota at around 34%, below the national norm — a mix of lower population density and slower wired-infrastructure buildout outside Sioux Falls.

What are the best options outside Sioux Falls?

In lower-coverage states like South Dakota, satellite (Starlink, HughesNet, Viasat) and fixed wireless are often the most realistic options outside the main metro area, since wired buildout hasn't reached as far.

Is South Dakota getting funding to expand broadband?

Like most lower-coverage states, South Dakota is eligible for BEAD (Broadband Equity, Access, and Deployment) federal funding aimed at extending service into unserved and underserved areas over the next several years.
