Frequently Asked Questions — Michigan Internet

What's the state of internet access in Michigan?

FCC Broadband Data Collection figures put availability in Michigan at around 48%, a middle-of-the-pack number — solid in Detroit but noticeably weaker once you leave the metro area.

How many internet providers cover Michigan?

About 6 major providers are reported active in Michigan per FCC data, concentrated mostly around Detroit and other population centers.

Is rural Michigan well-served for broadband?

Not as well as the metro areas — rural parts of Michigan commonly depend on fixed wireless or satellite service where wired broadband hasn't been built out.
