Types of Rural Internet

Satellite Internet

Available almost everywhere but may have higher latency.

Fixed Wireless

Uses radio signals from nearby towers. Faster than satellite in some areas.

DSL

Older technology but still available in some rural locations.

Why Internet is Limited in Rural Areas

Rural areas have fewer infrastructure investments, making fiber and cable less available. Satellite and wireless solutions help bridge this gap.
