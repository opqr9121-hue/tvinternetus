What is Fiber Internet?

Fiber internet uses light signals through fiber-optic cables, delivering extremely fast and reliable speeds. It is ideal for heavy users, gamers, and households with multiple devices.

What is Cable Internet?

Cable internet uses coaxial cables and is widely available across the U.S. It provides strong performance for most households at a lower cost than fiber.
