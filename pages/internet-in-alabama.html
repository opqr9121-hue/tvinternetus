Frequently Asked Questions — Alabama Internet

What's the state of internet access in Alabama?

FCC Broadband Data Collection figures put availability in Alabama at around 42%, a middle-of-the-pack number — solid in Birmingham but noticeably weaker once you leave the metro area.

How many internet providers cover Alabama?

About 3 major providers are reported active in Alabama per FCC data, concentrated mostly around Birmingham and other population centers.

Is rural Alabama well-served for broadband?

Not as well as the metro areas — rural parts of Alabama commonly depend on fixed wireless or satellite service where wired broadband hasn't been built out.
